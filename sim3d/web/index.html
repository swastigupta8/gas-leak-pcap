About this simulation

A GPU simulation of the styrene vapour release at LG Polymers, R. R. Venkatapuram, Visakhapatnam, on the night of 7 May 2020. The air is computed in 3D with the Lattice Boltzmann method over real terrain and buildings; the heavy vapour is carried by that air and sinks under its own weight.

What the colours mean

US EPA 60-minute exposure guidelines (AEGL): discomfort above AEGL-1, serious or lasting harm above AEGL-2, life-threatening above AEGL-3.
